About the College

Empowering Future Agriculturists Through Quality Education

ज्ञान से खेतों तक, नवाचार से भविष्य तक

Choudhary Parma Ram Godara Agriculture College, Bhadra is committed to quality agricultural education through academic excellence, practical learning and field-based experience. Our aim is to prepare skilled and responsible agriculture professionals for modern and sustainable farming.

हमारा उद्देश्य: विद्यार्थियों को गुणवत्तापूर्ण कृषि शिक्षा, व्यावहारिक ज्ञान एवं खेत आधारित अनुभव प्रदान कर आधुनिक एवं टिकाऊ कृषि के लिए कुशल, आत्मविश्वासी और जिम्मेदार कृषि पेशेवर तैयार करना।

Our Philosophy

Vision, Mission & Rural Commitment

ज्ञान खेतों तक • तकनीक किसानों तक • शिक्षा समृद्ध गाँवों तक

Our Vision / हमारा दृष्टिकोण

To develop skilled, responsible and innovative agriculture professionals who contribute to sustainable farming, rural development and the prosperity of farming communities.

Our Mission / हमारा मिशन

To provide quality agricultural education through academic excellence, practical training, field-based learning, modern technology and professional development.

Rural Development / ग्रामीण विकास

To strengthen the connection between agricultural education, farmers and villages through awareness, practical knowledge and sustainable rural development.

Our Core Values / हमारे मूल मूल्य

Academic Excellence • Practical Learning • Sustainability • Innovation • Farmer & Rural Connect • Responsibility & Ethics
